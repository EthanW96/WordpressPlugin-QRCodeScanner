# QR Code Tracker — Plugin Wiki

Welcome to the official documentation for the **QR Code Tracker** WordPress plugin, built for the Advent Tree Network.

This plugin lets you generate QR codes tied to physical tree locations, track scans in real time, manage teams, and integrate seamlessly with WooCommerce purchases.

## What This Plugin Does

1. **Generates QR codes** linked to a postcode + city + tree combination.
2. **Tracks every scan** — distinguishing direct scans, social shares, and legacy URL formats.
3. **Integrates with WooCommerce** to automatically create QR records when a tree product is purchased.
4. **Sends welcome emails** to buyers with their QR images, share links, and management links.
5. **Organizes QR codes into teams** so groups or organizations can manage their own trees.
6. **Provides detailed reports** with filters, breakdowns, rollups, and CSV exports.
